Focus test
  1. Turn on Focus Spoofer for this tab from the toolbar.
  2. Switch to another tab, or minimize the window, for a few seconds.
  3. Come back. No blur or visibility entries in the log means it's working.
Fingerprint test
  1. With the extension off, reload a few times. The three hashes stay the same.
  2. Turn it on and reload again. The hashes now change on every load.
  3. Turned fingerprint randomization off in Settings? Then they stay the same, by design.
