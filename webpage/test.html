    What a page can't see

    No web page can see which site you went to or read your other tabs. It only knows that it lost focus or was hidden, when, and for how long. The "How" and "Where" above are guesses from the last thing your mouse and keyboard did on this page before you left.

    Other signals exist that no browser extension can hide from every site, such as server-side activity timing. See the FAQ for what Focus Spoofer does and doesn't cover.
